# Coastal Climate Patterns

## Ocean Influence

Coastal regions generally experience smaller annual temperature variations than inland areas.

Large bodies of water absorb and release heat more slowly than land, moderating nearby air temperatures.

## Humidity

High humidity is common near warm coastal waters. Moisture transported from the ocean can contribute to cloud formation and precipitation.

## Sea Breezes

During sunny days, land can warm faster than the adjacent ocean. This temperature difference creates pressure gradients that drive sea breezes toward the coast.

## Storm Systems

Coastal weather can be strongly affected by tropical cyclones, frontal systems, and seasonal changes in ocean temperature.

Forecasting these events requires observations from satellites, weather stations, radar, and ocean monitoring systems.

## Long-Term Changes

Changes in sea surface temperature, ocean circulation, and atmospheric patterns can influence regional precipitation and extreme weather.

Long-term climate analysis relies on measurements collected over many years rather than isolated weather observations.
